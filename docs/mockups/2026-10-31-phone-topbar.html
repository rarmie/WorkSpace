Phone top bar, chosen 31 Oct 2026 (bottom tab bar)

The bar used to need 657 px, so on a 385 px phone the Logout button and the avatar ran off the edge. Chosen: the three pages move to a tab bar at the bottom of the screen, where a thumb reaches it, and the top bar keeps the logo and the buttons as icons (Logout loses its text). From 1024 px up (the lg breakpoint) nothing changes: the full bar needs 836 px, so a lower switch point overflowed. Under 1024 px, including tablets, the tab bar shows. Use Phone width to check a narrower phone.
